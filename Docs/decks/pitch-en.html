Where the tension comes from

Sanity isn't a health bar. It's a countdown.

Stay in the dark and it drops. The lower it gets, the more likely the ghost hunts you.

P = (50 − sanity) / 50

Sanity 50%Never hunts Sanity 25%Even odds Sanity 0%Always hunts

So every extra piece of evidence costs you. That trade is the whole game.
